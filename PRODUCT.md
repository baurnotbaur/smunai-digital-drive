## Platform

web

## Users

1. **Private motorists (Жезқазған, Сәтбаев, Астана)**: drivers seeking reliable, lab-certified fuel, round-the-clock service, convenience store & quality coffee (flagship at Ұлытау 4/2), and QR coupons.
2. **B2B fleet managers & corporate enterprises**: transport companies and businesses requiring corporate fuel cards, automated accounting, 16% VAT deduction, 7% drain prevention, and daily limit controls.
3. **Job seekers (HR candidates)**: local residents looking for long-term employment with stable shifts, transparent pay, and social guarantees (cashiers, station operators, fuel truck drivers).

## Product Purpose

The official digital platform and service ecosystem of the «S-Munai» gas station network. It connects local drivers with station locations and 24/7 services, provides an instant B2B fuel savings calculator with online card ordering, publishes verified career openings, and delivers AI-assisted customer support.

## Positioning

The authentic, trusted local gas station network with a 30-year heritage (founded in 1996), deeply rooted in the Ulytau region («Жанармай — көлікке, Ұлытау — жүректе»). Unlike younger retail competitors, S-Munai represents three decades of unbroken trust, certified refinery fuel, and family-level care for local motorists and enterprise fleets.

## Operating Context

8 operational stations across Kazakhstan:
- 3 in Zhezkazgan (including flagship Station №4 at Ұлытау 4/2 with 24/7 store and coffee, Mira 39, Ұлытау 5).
- 3 in Satpaev.
- 2 in Astana.
Stations operate 24/7, equipped with modern fuel accounting, Zebra DS2208 scanners for instant QR coupons, and direct delivery logistics from refineries.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Bilingual presentation**: Kazakh as the primary public language, Russian fully supported.
- **Pricing policy**: Retail fuel prices are never published in social media or public promotional banners; the B2B calculator provides an illustrative estimate based on official tax rules (16% VAT).
- **Legal compliance**: Full adherence to the Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan «On Personal Data and its Protection», with explicit consent checkboxes on all lead forms.
- **Design standard**: Apple-inspired clean elegance, restrained luxury, high scanability, no cluttered SaaS widgets.
